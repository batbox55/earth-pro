This independent Swift reconstruction uses no Google Earth application binaries, private imagery API, or bundled Google branding.

Country borders, country labels and cities: Natural Earth, 1:110m, public domain.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/master/geojson
Bundled datasets: ne_110m_admin_0_countries, ne_110m_populated_places_simple, ne_50m_admin_1_states_provinces_lines.
Weather: NASA GIBS MODIS Terra Cloud Fraction Day, daily observation (not a forecast).
https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov/
Gallery earthquakes: USGS M2.5+ past-seven-days GeoJSON summary feed.
https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/feed/v1.0/geojson.php
Photos: Wikimedia Commons. Individual author, license and original source are shown with each photo.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:API/MediaWiki
OpenStreetMap street tiles: © OpenStreetMap contributors.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

Water body outlines: Natural Earth 1:50m lakes and reservoirs, public domain. Polygon rings converted to lines, coordinates rounded to four decimals.
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_50m_lakes.geojson
Globe roads: Natural Earth 1:10m major roads, public domain; scale ranks 3-4, simplified at 0.015 degrees and rounded to four decimals. Generalized overview, not navigation data.
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_10m_roads.geojson
Wikipedia article excerpts: Wikipedia contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0; each article links to its source and revision history.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Historical imagery: NASA GIBS Terra / MODIS Corrected Reflectance (True Color), daily swaths from 2000-02-24; nominal source resolution 250 m. The globe preview is downsampled to 2048x1024. Clouds and gaps remain, and may appear blank. This is not Google's historical aerial archive.
https://nasa-gibs.github.io/gibs-api-docs/python-usage/
Terrain elevation: Copernicus DEM 2021 GLO-90, 90 m, via Open-Meteo Elevation API; approximate land surface elevation, not bathymetry. The free Open-Meteo endpoint is for non-commercial use.
https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/elevation-api
https://spacedata.copernicus.eu/collections/copernicus-digital-elevation-model
Sunlight: approximate solar declination and sidereal time; visualization only. Eye altitude uses a spherical Earth camera calculation, not terrain clearance.

Bundled globe terrain: Terrain Tiles / Mapzen via AWS Open Data, accessed 2026-10-16. Sixteen zoom-2 Terrarium tiles converted to a 1024x1024 signed-meter DEM. Coarse global land relief at true scale; ocean surface remains at sea level. Local map terrain comes from the map provider.
https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md
Parks and transportation: Natural Earth public-domain 1:10m parks/protected lands (387 U.S. NPS units in bundled dataset, with point/line/area features) and 893 selected airports worldwide. Park marker positions derived from area/line geometry are approximate. Generalized data, not for navigation.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/parks-and-protected-lands/
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/airports/
